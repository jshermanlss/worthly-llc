Know what it's worth.

Take a few photos. Worthly identifies the item, estimates its value, and writes a professional listing. Publish to your own eBay account when you are ready, or stop at the estimate.

Android app · package com.worthly.android
Published by Worthly LLC. Worthly is not operated or endorsed by eBay.

Built for the selling workflow

Photograph an item, review the draft, then decide whether it belongs on eBay. The Google Play listing describes the app this way.

01

Capture

Take photos or pick them from the gallery. The app uses those photos to draft a title, a description, and pricing guidance.

02

Assess or list

Ask for a value assessment without listing, or prepare an eBay listing. You can convert an assessment into a listing later.

03

Publish on your account

Connect an eBay seller account and publish when the draft is right. Edit drafts, update live listings, and end or republish them. Shipping choices include USPS, UPS, FedEx, local pickup, and freight.

The app

Screens from the current Google Play listing. Home tracks drafts, assessments, and live listings. Credits shows how analyses are spent.

Credits, bought once

Analyses spend credits. Publishing, updating, or ending a listing does not. Packs are one-time consumable purchases through Google Play Billing, recorded with RevenueCat. There is no subscription.
