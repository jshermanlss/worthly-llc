About

We built Worthly to help people find out what their stuff is worth and sell it with less hassle.

Who we are

Worthly is made by Worthly LLC, a small business based in Virginia.

We built this app to help people find out what their items are worth and sell them on eBay without the usual hassle of researching prices, writing listings, and figuring out shipping.

What we do

Worthly uses AI to identify items from photos, estimate their value based on recent market data, and write professional eBay listings. You can get a quick value assessment without selling, or create a complete listing ready to publish to your eBay account.

We charge credits only when you analyze an item. Editing, posting, or managing your listings is free. No subscription required.

Get in touch

We're happy to answer questions and help with any issues. Reach us at support@worthly-llc.com.
