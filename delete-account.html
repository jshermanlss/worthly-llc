Delete your Worthly account

Last updated October 31, 2026. App: Worthly - Know what it's worth, Android package com.worthly.android, published by Worthly LLC.

Worthly accounts are separate from eBay. You sign in with Google or with an email address and password. Linked eBay seller accounts and analysis credits sit on that Worthly account. This page is the public way to delete the account and the data Worthly LLC stores for it. You do not need to be signed in on this website.

Delete in the app

This is the fastest path. It runs when you confirm.

  1. Open Worthly on your device.
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap Account in the title bar.
  4. Tap Delete Worthly account and confirm.

That deletes the account on our servers: the email or Google identity, hashed sessions, the credit balance, and linked eBay sellers and tokens. On that device it also clears local assessments, preferences, drafts, photos, and eBay-linked data. Live eBay listings are not ended.

Request by email

If you cannot use the app, email support@worthly-llc.com with the subject Account deletion request and include:

  • The email address on the Worthly account, and
  • Any linked eBay username that helps us verify the request.

We complete a verified deletion request within 30 days. We may ask a follow-up question if we cannot match the account.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your user record: email or Google identity, hashed sessions, credit balance, and purchase identifiers we store.
  • Linked eBay seller records, including encrypted refresh tokens and stored policy ids.
  • On the device you use for in-app deletion: local assessments, preferences, drafts, photos, and other eBay-linked data stored on that device.

What we do not control, and what may remain

  • eBay listings and your eBay account stay on eBay. Deleting Worthly does not end live listings and does not close eBay. End listings in the app or in eBay Seller Hub first if you want them removed from eBay.
  • If eBay tells us a marketplace account was deleted, we unlink that seller only. Your Worthly account and credits stay until you delete them.
  • Google Play and Apple purchase records stay with those stores. Deleting Worthly does not refund a credit pack.
  • Security logs may be kept where we need them for security, fraud prevention, or the law. They are not used to keep providing the deleted account.

Sign out, unlink eBay, or delete

Difference between signing out, unlinking eBay, and deleting the Worthly account
Action What happens
Sign out Revokes this device’s session. The account, credits, and linked sellers remain.
Unlink eBay Removes that seller from the Worthly account. Credits stay. An eBay marketplace account-deletion notice unlinks that seller only.
Delete Worthly account Removes the user, all linked sellers, sessions, and credits, and clears related data on the device you are using. Live eBay listings stay up.
