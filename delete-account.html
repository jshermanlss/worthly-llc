Delete your Worthly account

Last updated October 31, 2026.

You can delete your Worthly account and its data at any time. Your eBay account is separate and won't be affected.

The fastest way:

  1. Open Worthly on your phone.
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap Account in the title bar.
  4. Tap Delete Worthly account and confirm.

What we delete

Your sign-in account, profile, credits, eBay connection, and uploaded listing photos on our servers, plus Worthly drafts and photos on that phone.

What we keep

A minimal purchase record with no payment details and no link to you, kept to handle refunds and prevent fraud, and server logs kept for up to 30 days.

We also keep a one-way marker so the free sign-up credits are not granted again. That marker does not store your email.

To fully disconnect eBay, also remove Worthly from your eBay account's app permissions.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. Deleting your account erases unused credits and doesn't trigger a refund.

If you can't use the app

Email support@worthly-llc.com with the subject Account deletion request. Include the email on your Worthly account and any eBay username. We delete a matched account within 30 days.

Sign out, unlink eBay, or delete

Difference between signing out, unlinking eBay, and deleting the Worthly account
Action What happens
Sign out Ends the session on this phone. Your account stays.
Unlink eBay Removes the eBay connection. Your credits stay.
Delete your account Removes the Worthly account and the data listed above. Your eBay account stays.
