Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026. This policy covers the Android app “Worthly - Know what it's worth” (package com.worthly.android) and the website worthly-llc.com. “We” and “us” mean Worthly LLC.

Worthly lets you photograph an item, generate an AI-assisted value assessment or eBay listing draft, and publish to an eBay seller account you connect. This policy says what information that takes, who receives it, and how to delete it.

Information we collect

Account

You sign in with Google or with an email address and password. We store your email, display name, a Firebase user id, and hashed app session tokens. Analysis credits live on the account and follow you to other devices where you sign in to the same account.

eBay connection

You may link one or more eBay seller accounts. We receive identifiers such as your eBay user id and username, and the OAuth tokens needed to list, update, and end listings on your behalf. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. Listing and publishing use the eBay account you mark as active.

Photos and listing content

We process images you capture or select, and the titles, descriptions, prices, category and item specifics, shipping details, and other draft fields you create. Drafts are kept on the device for each connected eBay account. Listing images may be uploaded to our storage so eBay can fetch them when you publish.

Location

With your permission, the app may use approximate or precise location for ship-from or regional pricing context. You can deny location permission. Some publish flows then ask you to enter a postal code instead.

Device, session, and diagnostics

We keep app session tokens on your device, and basic diagnostics such as request logs and error messages, plus technical metadata needed to run the service. We have not identified a separate advertising or product-analytics SDK in the materials this policy is based on.

How we use it

  • To analyze item photos and generate listing drafts and pricing guidance, using an AI provider. The service description we already publish names OpenAI.
  • To run Deep Research, which the app describes as a web search for recent sold prices with cited sources.
  • To upload listing images so eBay can fetch them when you publish.
  • To authenticate you, keep you signed in, and call eBay APIs you authorize.
  • To keep your credit balance and reduce abuse of free or purchased credits.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.
  • To comply with law and with platform rules, including eBay marketplace account-deletion notifications.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • eBay — listing content and the account actions you start.
  • Google — sign-in, and Google Cloud for hosting and storage of the backend the app uses. Google Play distributes the app.
  • OpenAI — image and text analysis used to draft listings and pricing guidance, as named in our existing service description.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play — to validate in-app purchases and tell our servers to grant credits. See Purchases below.
  • Authorities and safety — when the law requires it, or to protect users, eBay, or the service.

Google Play’s public Data safety summary says the app may collect and share location, personal info, and three other categories that the summary does not name on the store page. This policy is the fuller description. If a category is missing here, write to us and we will correct it.

Purchases

Paid credits are one-time consumable packs sold by Google Play Billing: 25, 100, or 500 credits (worthly_credits_25, worthly_credits_100, worthly_credits_500). RevenueCat validates the purchase. Our backend receives the purchase notification and stores the credit balance and the purchase identifiers needed to grant credits. We do not receive your full payment card number. A separate non-consumable Play product, worthly_prereg_10, is used only to deliver a pre-registration credit reward, not as a paid pack.

Google Play and RevenueCat process purchase data under their own policies. App store purchase records stay with Google even if you delete your Worthly account.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account, linked eBay credentials, and draft or listing data while the account is open and as needed to run the service. Listing images may remain in storage while they are tied to an active or recently published listing.

When you delete your Worthly account we delete the user record, linked sellers, sessions, and credits. Instructions are on the account deletion page. When eBay notifies us that a marketplace account was deleted, we unlink that eBay seller only. Your Worthly account and credits remain until you delete them.

After deletion we may keep listing images already uploaded for published listings for a short operational cleanup. They are not used to identify you after the account is gone. Security and legal logs may be kept for a limited time where we need them for security, fraud prevention, or law. Google Play purchase records remain with Google.

Your choices

  • Sign out to revoke that device’s session.
  • Unlink an eBay seller in Account without deleting credits.
  • Delete the Worthly account in the app (Account, then Delete Worthly account) or from the deletion page. Credits are deleted with the account.
  • Revoke the app in your eBay account settings.
  • Deny or turn off camera, photos, and location in the device settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The terms also require you to be at least 18 to create an account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email jsherman314@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

The app and this website use encrypted transport (HTTPS). eBay refresh tokens are encrypted at rest, and access to the backend is limited to operating the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International processing

We process information in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate, including Google, eBay, RevenueCat, and the AI provider. If you use the app from another country, you understand your information may be processed in those places.

This website

worthly-llc.com is a static site. Worthly LLC does not run accounts, cookies, or analytics on it, and the pages do not load third-party fonts or trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record ordinary connection logs for that hosting. Email you send us is received at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do. If a change materially reduces your rights, we will also take any extra step the law requires. Continued use of the app after the updated policy is posted means you accept the revision, except where the law requires a different form of notice or consent.
