Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026. This policy covers the Worthly Android app and worthly-llc.com. “We” and “us” mean Worthly LLC.

Worthly lets you photograph an item, see what it may be worth, and draft a listing. You can publish, update, or end listings on an eBay account you connect. This policy says what information that takes, who receives it, and how to delete it.

Information we collect

Account

You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. We keep the email, name, and credit balance on your account so you can stay signed in.

eBay

If you connect eBay, we store a secure, encrypted access key that lets Worthly post and manage listings for you. We also keep the eBay username so you can see which account is connected.

Photos and listings

We use the photos you take and the listing details Worthly drafts, such as the title, description, and price. Photos you upload for a listing are hosted at a web link so eBay can display them; anyone with that link can view them. We delete your uploaded listing photos when you delete your account. Photos uploaded before November 2026 may need a separate request; email support@worthly-llc.com and we'll remove them. Drafts and photos also stay on your phone until you remove them or delete your account.

Location

If you allow it, Worthly can use your location while you are using the app to fill in a ship-from address. You can say no and type a postal code instead.

Camera

Worthly uses the camera so you can photograph items. It does not use the microphone, and it does not ask to browse all the photos on your phone.

Diagnostics

Our servers keep basic logs to keep the service running. The app has no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • OpenAI processes the photos and text you submit to produce analyses: what the item is, what it may be worth, and a listing draft.
  • If you ask for one, OpenAI can also make an optional styled photo of the item.
  • Deep Research also checks recent sold prices on eBay, WorthPoint, LiveAuctioneers, and Invaluable, and includes links to those sources.
  • To host listing photos so eBay can display them, and to post or update listings when you ask.
  • To keep you signed in and to keep your credit balance.
  • To run, secure, and fix the app.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • eBay — the listing and the actions you start, such as posting, updating, or ending it.
  • Google — sign-in, hosting for the app and listing photos, and Google Play, which distributes Worthly and handles payment.
  • OpenAI — photos and text, so Worthly can produce analyses and an optional styled photo.
  • Google Play — credit-pack purchases. We learn that a purchase succeeded so we can add the credits. We do not receive your card number.
  • Authorities and safety — when the law requires it, or to protect users, eBay, or the service.

Purchases

Paid credits are one-time packs in Google Play: 25 credits for $4.99, 100 for $17.99, and 500 for $84.99 in the United States. You pay the price shown at checkout. We do not receive your card number. New accounts start with 10 free credits. If you pre-registered on Google Play, you may receive 10 more credits after you install the app and sign in. That reward is not a paid pack.

Google Play keeps its own purchase records under its policy, even if you delete your Worthly account.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and listing information while the account is open.

When you delete your account we delete your sign-in account, profile, credits, and eBay connection. We delete your uploaded listing photos when you delete your account. Photos uploaded before November 2026 may need a separate request; email support@worthly-llc.com and we'll remove them. On that phone we also clear Worthly drafts and photos. Your eBay account is separate and is not affected. Instructions are on the account deletion page.

We keep a minimal purchase record with no payment details and no link to you, so we can handle refunds and prevent fraud, and server logs for up to 30 days. We also keep a one-way marker so the free sign-up credits are not granted again. That marker does not store your email.

Google Play’s own purchase records stay with Google.

Your choices

  • Sign out on this phone. Your account stays.
  • Unlink an eBay seller without deleting credits.
  • Delete the Worthly account in the app: open Account in the title bar, tap Delete Worthly account, and confirm. You can also use the deletion page. Unused credits are erased and are not refunded.
  • To fully disconnect eBay, also remove Worthly from your eBay account’s app permissions.
  • Turn off camera or location in your phone settings. You can type a postal code instead of sharing location.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by emailing support@worthly-llc.com.

Children

Worthly is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, email support@worthly-llc.com and we will delete it.

Security

Worthly and this website use encrypted connections. The key that connects eBay is stored encrypted, and access to our systems is limited to running the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International processing

We process information in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate, including Google, eBay, and OpenAI. If you use the app from another country, your information may be processed in those places.

This website

worthly-llc.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Worthly LLC does not run accounts, cookies, or analytics on it, and the pages do not load third-party fonts or trackers. GitHub may record ordinary connection logs for that hosting. Email you send us is received at support@worthly-llc.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we change the date at the top of this page.
