Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026. This policy covers the Android app “Worthly - Know what it's worth” (package com.worthly.android) and the website worthly-llc.com. “We” and “us” mean Worthly LLC, the operator of the app.

Worthly lets you photograph an item, identify it, estimate its value, and draft a marketplace listing. You can publish, update, or end listings on eBay seller accounts you connect. This policy says what information that takes, who receives it, and how to delete it.

Information we collect

Account

You sign in with Google or with an email address and password through Firebase Authentication. We store your Firebase user id, email, display name, hashed session tokens, and credit balance in Cloud Firestore. After you sign in, our server issues a Worthly session token for later requests.

eBay connection

You may link one or more eBay seller accounts with eBay OAuth. We store the eBay user id, username, and policy ids, and the refresh token needed to list, update, and end listings on your behalf. Refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Listing and publishing use the eBay account you mark as active.

Photos and listing content

We process images you capture and the titles, descriptions, prices, categories, item specifics, shipping details, and other draft fields the app creates. Images you upload are stored in Google Cloud Storage so eBay can fetch them when you publish. Drafts and photos are also kept on your device in a local database.

Location

With your permission, the app may use fine or coarse location, only while the app is in use, for the eBay ship-from address and shipping calculation. You can deny location permission and enter a postal code instead.

Camera and other device permissions

The app asks for the camera so you can photograph items. It does not request the microphone, and it does not request broad access to read your media library.

Diagnostics

The service keeps basic diagnostics: request logs and error messages needed to run and troubleshoot it. The app does not include a product-analytics or crash-reporting SDK. There is no Sentry, Firebase Crashlytics, or Firebase Analytics package in the app.

How we use it

  • To identify an item from photos, estimate its value, and write a listing draft. That analysis is done with OpenAI.
  • To stage a lifestyle image of an item, also with OpenAI, when you ask for one.
  • To run Deep Research, which uses OpenAI web search limited to ebay.com, worthpoint.com, liveauctioneers.com, and invaluable.com.
  • To store listing images so eBay can fetch them when you publish, and to call eBay APIs you authorize.
  • To authenticate you, keep you signed in, and keep your credit balance.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.
  • To comply with law and with eBay’s marketplace account-deletion notices. When eBay sends one, we unlink that seller only.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • eBay — listing content and the account actions you start, through eBay’s selling APIs.
  • Google — Firebase Authentication, and Google Cloud for the backend (Cloud Run), listing images (Cloud Storage), and account records (Firestore). Google Play distributes the Android app.
  • OpenAI — photos and listing text sent for identification, valuation, listing copy, lifestyle image staging, and Deep Research.
  • RevenueCat and the app stores — to validate in-app purchases and grant credits. Android purchases use Google Play Billing. Purchases in the iOS app are billed by Apple.
  • Authorities and safety — when the law requires it, or to protect users, eBay, or the service.

Purchases

Paid credits on Android are one-time consumable packs sold by Google Play Billing: 25 credits for $4.99, 100 credits for $17.99, and 500 credits for $84.99 in the United States (worthly_credits_25, worthly_credits_100, worthly_credits_500). RevenueCat validates the purchase. Our server receives the purchase notification and stores the credit balance and the purchase identifiers needed to grant credits. We do not receive your full payment card number. A separate Play product, worthly_prereg_10, delivers a 10-credit pre-registration reward. It is not a paid pack.

Google Play, Apple, and RevenueCat process purchase data under their own policies. Store purchase records stay with the store even if you delete your Worthly account.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account, linked eBay credentials, and listing data while the account is open and as needed to run the service.

When you delete your Worthly account we delete the user record, the email or Google identity, hashed sessions, the credit balance, and linked eBay sellers and tokens. On the device you use, that deletion also clears local assessments, preferences, drafts, photos, and eBay-linked data stored on that device. Live eBay listings are not ended. Instructions are on the account deletion page.

When eBay notifies us that a marketplace account was deleted, we unlink that eBay seller only. Your Worthly account and credits remain until you delete them.

Google Play and Apple purchase records remain with those companies. Security logs may be kept where we need them for security, fraud prevention, or the law. They are not used to keep providing a deleted account.

Your choices

  • Sign out to revoke that device’s session.
  • Unlink an eBay seller without deleting credits.
  • Delete the Worthly account in the app: open Account in the title bar, tap Delete Worthly account, and confirm. You can also use the deletion page. Credits are deleted with the account.
  • Revoke the app in your eBay account settings.
  • Deny or turn off camera and location in the device settings. You can type a postal code instead of sharing location.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email support@worthly-llc.com and we will delete it.

Security

The app and this website use encrypted transport (HTTPS). eBay refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and access to the backend is limited to operating the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International processing

We process information in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate, including Google, eBay, RevenueCat, Apple, and OpenAI. If you use the app from another country, your information may be processed in those places.

This website

worthly-llc.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Worthly LLC does not run accounts, cookies, or analytics on it, and the pages do not load third-party fonts or trackers. GitHub may record ordinary connection logs for that hosting. Email you send us is received at support@worthly-llc.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do. If a change materially reduces your rights, we will also take any extra step the law requires. Continued use of the app after the updated policy is posted means you accept the revision, except where the law requires a different form of notice or consent.
